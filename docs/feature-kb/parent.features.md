### Fee Payment

- Key: `manual:fee-payment`
- Confidence: high
- Documentation from: manual

```text
Fee Payment
Where: Press the Pay Fees button on your Home page. It is not a separate row in the left menu.
What it is: Where you see your child's fee details and pay the school fees online.
How to use it: (1) Open Home and press Pay Fees. (2) There are two options at the top. Payment Gateway is for paying what is due now. Finance (Easy Monthly Installments) is for paying in smaller parts. (3) On Payment Gateway, check the Student Information, so you know the fees are for the right child. (4) Under Fee Particulars you see the Due Amount and the fee items, such as Course, Transport, Uniform, Material and Other amounts. For an exam fee, choose the exam with Select Exam. (5) Enter the amount you want to pay, then press Proceed For Payment and finish the payment in the secure window. (6) When it works you see Payment successful, and a receipt is emailed to you. Your payments are listed under Payment history.
Good to know: You must enter at least one amount before you can proceed. If it says Payment is being confirmed, your bank is still processing it. Do not pay again. The record updates as soon as it is confirmed, and you can press Check on that payment to look again. If it says Payment failed, no money was recorded, so you can safely try again. If you cancel, your fee request is saved as pending and you can finish paying later from your dashboard. Card details are handled by the payment gateway. The school never sees or stores them. If online payment is not available, it tells you to contact the school office.
```
