## Adaptive Assessment vs. Traditional Exams

Assessments are how we measure what a student knows — but the format of
that assessment matters more than it might seem.

## Traditional Exams

A traditional exam presents the same fixed set of questions to every
student, regardless of ability. This makes grading and comparison simple,
but it means a strong student may not be challenged, while a struggling
student may face questions far beyond their current level.

## Adaptive Assessments

An adaptive assessment adjusts the difficulty of questions in real time
based on how a student answers. Answer correctly, and the next question
gets harder; struggle, and it adjusts to find the right level — giving a
more precise read on what a student actually knows.

## Why It Matters for Accuracy

Because adaptive assessments hone in on a student's actual level, they can
often measure ability more precisely in fewer questions than a fixed-form
test.

## FAQs

**Are adaptive assessments harder than traditional exams?**
Not inherently — they're designed to match difficulty to the student's
level, which means they can feel more appropriately challenging rather
than uniformly hard or easy.

**Can adaptive assessments be used alongside traditional exams?**
Yes. Many schools use adaptive assessments for ongoing practice and
progress checks, while still using traditional exams for formal grading.
